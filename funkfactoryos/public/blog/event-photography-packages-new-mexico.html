What "Edited Images" Actually Means

Not all editing is the same. There are photographers in New Mexico who deliver "all photos" and send you 4,000 unprocessed RAW files in a Dropbox folder. That's not editing — that's data transfer.

When FFMG delivers edited images, we mean:

  • Color corrected — exposure, white balance, and contrast normalized across the full set. No blown highlights, no crushed blacks.
  • Selectively processed — we go through every frame and keep the ones worth keeping. Action sequences, reactions, candids. The discards are discards for a reason.
  • Batch processed — not individually retouched, but consistently graded so the set looks like one project, not 30 different lighting conditions.

For sports photography specifically, we do keywording and tagging — player numbers, team names, action type. That makes the gallery searchable for coaches and athletic directors who need to find specific moments fast.

What Affects Pricing in New Mexico

Event photography rates in New Mexico vary based on a few specific factors — and it's worth being transparent about what drives the numbers:

Coverage Duration

Most quotes start with hours of coverage. A two-hour corporate event is priced differently than a full-day conference or a season contract. Longer coverage means more shots, more editing, and more gallery management — all priced accordingly.

Turnaround Requirements

If you need images within 24 hours of the event (same-day recap for a social event, press deadline for a news cycle), that requires a dedicated workflow and priority processing. It's absolutely doable — just tell us upfront so we can staff accordingly.

Deliverables Beyond Photography

Video, social cuts, print files, re-edits for different formats — each add-on adds to the scope. Most clients come to us for photography and discover they also need formatted social content. We handle both, so you're not managing two vendors.

Location and Travel

We serve Albuquerque, Rio Rancho, Santa Fe, Las Cruces, and the broader New Mexico region. Events within 60 miles of Albuquerque have no travel fee. Events further out can be quoted on request — distance and duration determine the number.

What to Ask Before Booking Any Photographer in New Mexico

Before you sign a contract, run through this checklist:

  • How many images will I receive? If they can't give you a range, that's a red flag.
  • What's included in "editing"? Batch vs. selective processing makes a massive difference in quality.
  • What's the delivery timeline? "Soon" is not a timeline.
  • How long is the gallery available? Some photographers take galleries down in 30 days — that makes it impossible for athletes, coaches, and families to access their photos later.
  • Do I get full commercial rights? For schools, businesses, and organizations — you need to be able to use the images without licensing restrictions. Ask upfront.
  • Can I get formatted social versions? If you need to post to Instagram or Facebook, you want ready-to-use formats, not a cropped version of a horizontal photo.
