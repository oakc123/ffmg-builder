Shipping Policy 📦

We keep it simple — quality products, fast-as-we-can delivery, and no hidden fees. Here's everything you need to know.

🚚

Delivery Times

We work with trusted suppliers and ship from US-based warehouses whenever possible. Because we source the best products from around the world, delivery times vary slightly depending on the item.

Standard Shipping
7–15 Business Days
Most orders arrive in 7–10 days
Order Processing
1–3 Business Days
Up to 5 days during peak seasons

We're a small, curated shop — not a giant warehouse. Our products are hand-picked and sometimes ship from specialty suppliers, which is why delivery can take a bit longer than Amazon Prime. But trust us, the quality is worth the wait.

💰

Shipping Cost

We keep shipping affordable so you can spoil your pet without spoiling your wallet.

🎉 FREE shipping on all orders over $50! Automatically applied at checkout.

For orders under $50, standard shipping is a flat rate of $5.99 — no surprises, no weight-based calculations, no nonsense.

📍

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with tracking information so you can follow your package every step of the way.

You'll also get a confirmation email immediately after placing your order. If you don't see it, check your spam folder — our emails are friendly, we promise.

If your tracking hasn't updated in 5+ business days, drop us a line at hello@funkfactorypets.com and we'll investigate.

🇺🇸

Where We Ship

We currently ship within the United States only — all 50 states, including Alaska, Hawaii, PO Boxes, and APO/FPO addresses.

International shipping is on our roadmap. If you're outside the US and dying to get your paws on our stuff, email us at hello@funkfactorypets.com — we might be able to work something out.

💬

Questions?

We're real people who love pets. If you have questions about your shipment, need to update your address, or just want to show us a photo of your pet (we love that), email us anytime:

📧 hello@funkfactorypets.com — We typically respond within 24 hours.
