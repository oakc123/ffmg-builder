Returns & Refunds ↩️

Not vibing with your order? No worries. We make returns painless because happy pets (and pet parents) come first.

📅

30-Day Return Window

You have 30 days from the date of delivery to request a return or exchange on any product. Whether your pet turned their nose up at it or the item arrived damaged — we've got you covered.

🐾 30 days. No receipts. No drama. Just email us and we'll handle it.

Items should be in their original condition when possible — but we understand pets aren't exactly gentle. If the product is defective or damaged, we'll accept the return regardless.

📝

How to Request a Return

We keep it simple. Here's how to get started:

1
Email Us
Send an email to hello@funkfactorypets.com with your order number and a brief description of the issue. Photos help if the item is damaged.
2
Get Approved
We'll review your request and respond within 24 hours with return instructions. Most returns are approved same-day.
3
Ship It Back
Send the item back using the instructions we provide. For defective or damaged products, we'll cover the return shipping.
4
Get Your Refund
Once we receive and inspect the return, your refund is processed. Simple as that.
💸

🔄

Exchanges

Want a different size, color, or product? We handle exchanges the same way as returns — just let us know what you'd like instead when you email us.

If the replacement item costs more, we'll send you a payment link for the difference. If it costs less, we'll refund the difference to your original payment method.

⚠️

Non-Returnable Items

For hygiene and safety reasons, a few product categories cannot be returned once opened:

• Pet food and treats — unless the item arrived damaged or expired
• Personalized/custom items — made just for your pet

Everything else? Fair game for returns within our 30-day window.
