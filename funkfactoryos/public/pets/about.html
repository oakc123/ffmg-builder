💜 Our Story

Where every pet's a rockstar

It started with a golden retriever named Miso and a cat named Pickles who had extremely bad taste in toys. Everything in the pet store either fell apart in a week or cost as much as rent.

We knew there had to be a better way. So we scoured the internet, found the best-reviewed, most-fun, most-durable pet products we could — things pets actually love — and made them easy to find in one place.

Funk Factory Pets was born. No fluff, no filler. Just great pet gear at fair prices, delivered to your door.
