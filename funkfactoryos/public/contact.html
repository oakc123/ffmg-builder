Common Questions

Before you reach out

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend reaching out at least 2–4 weeks before your event. For sports season packages, contact us 4–6 weeks ahead. That said — if you've got something coming up sooner, still reach out. We'll tell you what's possible.

Do you travel outside Albuquerque?

No travel fee within 60 miles of Albuquerque (Rio Rancho, Santa Fe, Tijeras, Moriarty). Events further out can be arranged — travel fees depend on distance and duration. Ask in your inquiry and we'll give you a straight answer.

What's your turnaround time?

Standard recap delivery is 48 hours after the event. For sports season packages, galleries go out every Sunday morning. Brand films and web projects have their own timelines discussed during kickoff. We don't miss deadlines — it's the core of the reputation.

Can I get raw files or all the footage?

We deliver edited, curated work — not raw dumps. Raw footage is available as an add-on for select projects. Our edit is the product: we frame the story, color-grade, and deliver what's worth keeping.

Do you offer ongoing retainer packages?

Yes. We offer monthly content retainers for venues, schools, and organizations that need consistent coverage. Monthly rates, scheduled shoot days, and predictable output. See web design retainers here →
