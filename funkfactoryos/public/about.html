Funk Factory Media Group started as most creative businesses do — one camera, one passion, and a willingness to show up. We were in the stands before we were on the sidelines. We shot every weekend game, every local concert, every community event we could find.

Over time, the craft sharpened and the clients followed. Schools, venues, leagues, and artists across New Mexico started calling on FFMG when they needed media that captured what actually happened — not a sanitized marketing version of it.

"Great imagery isn't just marketing — it's the record. We treat every shot like it might be the one that gets framed."

We're based in Albuquerque, but we travel throughout New Mexico — Albuquerque, Santa Fe, Rio Rancho, Las Cruces, and everywhere in between. If there's a game, a show, or an event worth capturing, we'll be there.

In 2020, we expanded beyond photography and videography into web design — building digital platforms for the same clients who needed their media to live somewhere worth showing it. The same principles apply: clear, purposeful, built to last.

How We Work

What we believe about good media

01

Show up prepared

We scout venues before game day. We know the lighting before the first pitch. Preparation is what separates a great shot from a lucky one — and we don't rely on luck.

02

Deliver on time, every time

Coaches need film before Monday practice. Venues need content before the post goes live. We have a 48-hour turnaround on recaps because your clients' timelines are real, and so are ours.

03

Authentic energy only

We don't stage shots. We don't use stock. We capture the real moment — the celebration, the exhaustion, the intensity — because that's what people actually connect with.

04

No junk shots in the gallery

Quantity is easy. We edit with taste. You'll never scroll through 800 blurry frames wondering which one to use — the edit is part of the product.

05

Know your client's world

We've been covering sports long enough to know what a coach needs vs. what a booster club wants vs. what the athlete's parents are going to frame. That context changes every shot decision.

06

Build to last

Whether it's a photo gallery or a website, we build things that don't break in three months. Every deliverable is made to work — not just to impress in the initial handoff.
